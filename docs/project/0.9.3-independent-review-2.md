### F10 detail

The change batches plain text in `append_escaped_text`. It runs only when the
line prefix is inactive. In that state, `advance()` leaves every field
unchanged for a plain character. The characters that need an escape are then
exactly the scan-boundary set. The budget check uses the same byte count as
the per-character path. The new equivalence test compares the batched writer
with the reference writer across fragments that cross line starts, headings,
tables, and inline delimiters. The full Rust suite passes.

## 7. Writing Style

The review applied the ASD-STE100 Issue 9 subset that the repository can check
or review by hand. The subset covers these rules:

- Descriptive sentences of 25 words or fewer.
- Procedural sentences of 20 words or fewer.
- No semicolons, contractions, or Latin abbreviations.
- Active voice where the actor is known.

The new English documents pass the automated audit after the two fixes above.
The dictionary clause remains a human review item. This document claims no
certification.

## 8. Bilingual Pair

`README.md` and `README_zh-CN.md` now agree on the version state. They also
agree on the attribution of the final breaking release, the 0.9.3 scope, and
the Ubuntu architecture coverage. The install paths and the links match, and
so do the generated matrix sections.

## 9. Verdict

**NO-GO for publication.** No open High finding remains in the product,
packaging, or documentation. The two open items are gate conditions:

1. The four container-registry blocking rows need their records from
   `official-docker-release-gate` after the fuzz qualification.
2. The evidence must bind the frozen candidate SHA, not an earlier one.

Release only after a run at the frozen SHA passes with `require_blocking`
enabled and covers all 48 blocking rows.
